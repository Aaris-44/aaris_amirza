What I'm working on

Sustaining NFT practice in the Malaysian creative industry

PhD research · Universiti Teknologi MARA

This study examines Malaysia's NFT ecosystem after the market downturn: who takes part, why creators adopt or withdraw, and what conditions support long-term use. The outcome is a sustainability framework validated by expert review.
